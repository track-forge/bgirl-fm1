SH-ISH

Two real-capture translations using ANALOG. Single-track approximations, not a Roland emulator: mixed waves, stored chords and source timing laws are not reproduced.

Select any of six tracks with ALGORITHM, then load. On the panel: PRESETS → LIST → SH-ISH for Crunchy Bass and Velvet Pulse. These sounds show SH-ISH in place of ANALOG; the original ANALOG sounds stay unchanged. Edit, play and save normally; loading keeps the track’s pattern, mixer and chord-key settings. No demo or saved-slot replacement.
